# What to test

1. With a clean Windows profile, try cloud text chat without a local model; then try installing Ollama or detecting LM Studio.
2. Try cancelling before and during a download, then retry after a connection interruption. Use the installer’s own Cancel action once it opens.
3. Play RU, EN, and JA voice samples, restore Ono_Anna, and try releasing and reloading resources. The app should not unload a resource unexpectedly during a conversation.
4. Switch between all three interface languages and test a small window at 125–200% display scaling. With an existing 0.6.8.1 profile, check history, memory, language, and that setup does not reopen.
5. Preview a diagnostic report, exclude a field or group, then save or copy it. Look for accidental keys, private chats or paths, audio, and screenshots. Nothing is submitted automatically.

Include the app version, Windows version, hardware category, expected and observed behavior, and steps to reproduce. Do not send credentials. [Email](mailto:sumirehelp@gmail.com) · [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/AzazeliusZ/Sumire-Closed-Beta/issues).
